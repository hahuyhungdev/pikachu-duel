PikachuDuel

The classic tile game, played by two people at once. You each get the same board, side by side — clear yours before your opponent clears theirs.

The host picks the board, then both of you get the same deal.

  • Pick two matching animals. They clear if a path can join them with at most two turns.
  • The path may only cross empty space — cleared tiles, or the gap around the outside of the board.
  • Player 1 plays the left board: click, or WASD + Space, hint Q, shuffle E.
  • Player 2 plays the right board: click, or ↑←↓→ + Enter, hint ,, shuffle ..
  • Every tile has the same face, so only the picture tells them apart — no colour shortcuts.
  • Consecutive matches build a streak worth extra points. Run the clock out and the most pairs wins.
Both boards are dealt from the same seed — nobody gets an easier grid.
